- **Loop Generation**: Seamless loop creation

#### **Style Transfer & Adaptation**

- **Camera Angles**: Wide shot, medium shot, close-up, extreme close-up

#### **Script-to-Video Conversion**
- **Screenplay Parsing**: Automatic scene detection and description extraction
- **Character Recognition**: Identify characters and assign visual traits
- **Dialogue Timing**: Synchronize video with audio narration
- **Scene Transitions**: Automatic transition generation
- **Visual Consistency**: Maintain character and location consistency
- **Pacing Analysis**: Adjust timing based on script rhythm

#### **Content Expansion**
- **Transition Generation**: Fade, wipe, dissolve, morph transitions
